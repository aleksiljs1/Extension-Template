FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I get Pro after paying?

Your license key arrives by email right after checkout. In Gmail, open Templates → Manage, paste the key under Plan and click Activate. Pro unlocks instantly.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time payment for a lifetime license. You can use it on up to 3 browsers or computers.

Which browsers does it work in?

Any Chromium browser that installs Chrome Web Store extensions: Google Chrome, Brave and Microsoft Edge, on Windows, Mac and Linux. It works in Gmail on the web.

Where are my templates stored?

Locally, in your browser's extension storage. They are never uploaded anywhere. Use Export to back them up or move them to another computer.

Can I share a template with my team?

Yes. Click Export on any template to get a small file. Your colleague clicks Import and has it instantly.

What if it's not for me?

Email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund, no questions asked. See the refund policy.
