Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Eztemplate ("the extension"), made by Cilicom, lets you save HTML email templates and insert them into Gmail drafts. This policy explains what data it handles.

What we collect

Nothing. Eztemplate does not collect, sell or share your emails, your templates or any personal data, and does not send them to us or anyone else.

What stays on your computer

  • Your templates and drafts (name, subject and email content, including images you add).
  • Your Gmail address, used only as a label so each Gmail account has its own template list.
  • Small settings, such as whether the editor opens full screen, and your Pro license key if you have one.

All of this is stored in your browser's extension storage on your device. It never leaves your computer unless you choose Export, which saves a file that only you control.

Gmail access

The extension runs only on mail.google.com. It reads the draft you are writing only to place a template where your cursor is, and fills in the subject line if your template has one. It does not read your inbox or other emails.

Pro licenses and payments

Purchases are handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details from Dodo Payments so we can provide support and refunds; we never see your card details. When you activate Pro, the extension sends your license key and a short device label (for example "Gmail · Brave · Windows") to Dodo Payments' license server to confirm the key is valid, and re-checks it about once a week. No email content or templates are ever sent.

Permissions

  • storage / unlimitedStorage: to save your templates (with images) on your device.
  • mail.google.com: to show the Templates button and insert templates into drafts.
  • Dodo Payments license server: only to check a Pro license key you enter.

Children

Eztemplate is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, mention it in the extension's update notes.
